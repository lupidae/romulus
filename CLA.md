The CLA assistant asks you on your first pull request. It comments on the
pull request with a link to this document and the sentence to reply with;
replying with it signs this Agreement for your current and future
Contributions. The CLA assistant records your account, the date and the
version of this Agreement you signed. The pull request cannot be merged until
