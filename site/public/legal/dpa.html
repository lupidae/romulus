This Data Processing Agreement (the "DPA") forms part of the romulus Terms of Service at https://romulus.lupidae.com/legal/terms (the "Terms") between:

  • the Customer, being a Business Customer or a Non-professional within the meaning of the Terms, acting as controller, or as processor on behalf of its own customers, in which case romulus acts as its sub-processor; and
  • Lucas Lefort, entrepreneur individuel (EI), trading as "romulus", 108 rue de Gand, 59200 Tourcoing, France, SIREN 949 815 138, romulus@lupidae.com ("romulus"), acting as processor.

This DPA is concluded electronically when the Customer accepts the Terms as a business, and it applies to every Organization the Customer holds. It does not apply to Consumers. On request to romulus@lupidae.com, romulus sends the Customer a copy of this DPA signed by romulus. A French version of the Terms and of the Privacy Policy is available; this DPA is provided in English.

1. Definitions

Terms defined in Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the "GDPR"), such as "personal data", "processing", "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data breach" and "supervisory authority", have the meaning given in the GDPR. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Terms. In addition:

  • "Customer Personal Data": personal data that romulus processes on behalf of the Customer, namely the personal data contained in Customer Content (schema definitions, drafts, versions, migrations, comments, approvals, Playground queries and migrations) and in the audit log of the Customer's Organizations.
  • "Data Protection Law": the GDPR, French Law No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 ("Loi Informatique et Libertés"), and any other data protection law of the European Union or of its Member States that applies to the processing.
  • "Subprocessor": any processor engaged by romulus that processes Customer Personal Data.
  • "SCCs": the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.

2. Scope and roles

2.1. romulus is designed to process little Customer Personal Data: it never connects to the Customer's databases, it stores schema definitions rather than database rows, and Playgrounds are filled with generated fake data. This DPA applies to the extent that Customer Content or the audit log nevertheless contains personal data, for example in identifiers, default values, check constraints, comments, names of authors and approvers, or values typed into Playground queries.

2.2. For Customer Personal Data, the Customer is the controller and romulus is the processor.

2.3. This DPA does not apply to the personal data that romulus processes as a controller, namely the Account, session, Organization membership, invitation, billing, terms acceptance, security and support data relating to the Customer's users. That processing is described in the Privacy Policy at https://romulus.lupidae.com/legal/privacy.

2.4. The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of personal data and the categories of data subjects are described in Annex 1.

4.4. Data subject requests. Taking into account the nature of the processing, romulus assists the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as possible, in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights. 5.2. Where it is not possible to provide all the information at once, it is provided in phases without undue further delay.

5.3. romulus takes reasonable steps to contain and remedy the breach and to limit its effects. Notifying the supervisory authority and the data subjects remains the Customer's responsibility as controller, with romulus's assistance.

6.2. 8.1. romulus makes available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR, including this DPA, its annexes and answers to reasonable security questionnaires.

8.2. 9.3. romulus keeps Customer Personal Data after that only where European Union or Member State law requires it, in which case it remains bound by this DPA.

9.4. On request, romulus confirms the deletion in writing.

Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

1. Encryption in transit and network exposure

  • All connections between users or CI systems and the Service use TLS, which ends at Cloudflare's edge. The website sends HTTP Strict Transport Security.
  • Traffic between Cloudflare and the Service's servers goes through an encrypted Cloudflare tunnel opened from the servers. The servers expose no inbound port to the Internet.
  • The API only answers requests that come through the website's edge, which adds a shared secret key.
  • Emails are sent to the email provider, and data to Stripe, over TLS.
  • Because TLS ends at Cloudflare, Cloudflare technically processes the traffic in clear at its edge. It is therefore listed as a Subprocessor.

2. Playground isolation

  • Each Playground runs in its own container inside a gVisor sandbox, which intercepts system calls in a user-space kernel and separates the Playground from the host kernel.
  • Playground containers have no network access, a read-only root file system, no Linux capabilities and no privilege escalation. Each is limited to one processor, 512 MiB of memory without swap, 128 processes, and a disk quota of 1 or 2 GiB depending on its size.
  • Queries are limited to 30 seconds for reviewers and 120 seconds for developers; a migration run is limited to 10 minutes and 500 statements. The interactive console connects with a read-only role.
  • Playgrounds are separate database instances, distinct from the Service's main database and from each other. They are filled with generated fake data, stop after 15 minutes without activity, are deleted 7 days after their last use, and are never backed up.
  • The Playground database server logs no statements. Resource usage is monitored to detect abuse.

3. Access control within the Service

  • Sign-in through a self-hosted identity service (Zitadel), using OpenID Connect with PKCE. Accounts are locked after 10 failed attempts.
  • Sessions expire after 7 days without use and at most 30 days after sign-in. Session identifiers are stored only as hashes. Passwords are stored only as hashes by the identity service, whose keys are encrypted.
  • Role-based access control within each Organization (administrator, developer, reviewer). Every request is checked against the caller's membership of the Organization and role.
  • API tokens for CI are issued per database, with a scope, stored only as hashes, and can be revoked at any time.
  • Rate limits protect sign-in and the main features against abuse.

4. Administrative access

  • Administrative access to the infrastructure is limited to a small number of named persons, bound by confidentiality, and is used only to operate, secure and support the Service.
  • Secrets are kept outside the source code, in files with restricted permissions on the servers.
  • Physical access to the hardware is controlled by the hosting provider.

5. Backups and resilience

  • The Service's databases are backed up every day. Each backup is checked after it is written and kept for 14 days. A backup is also taken before each deployment.
  • Restore tests are carried out and their results recorded.
  • Removed versions remain restorable for 30 days.

6. Logging and monitoring

  • The application logs requests with their method, path (with tokens redacted) and identifier. It does not log the content of schemas, queries or comments.
  • Each Organization has an audit log of sensitive actions, which cannot be modified or deleted through the application.
  • Technical and security logs are kept for at most 6 months.

7. Secure development and operations

  • The source code is public (AGPL-3.0), which allows external review. The Service runs container images built by continuous integration from identified commits, with pinned versions of its components.
  • Vulnerabilities can be reported to romulus@lupidae.com under the security policy at https://github.com/lupidae/romulus/blob/main/SECURITY.md, which sets correction targets according to severity.

8. Organisational measures

  • Confidentiality obligations for every person with access to Customer Personal Data.
  • Written agreements under Article 28 GDPR with Subprocessors.
  • A personal data breach procedure in line with Section 5.
  • Data minimisation by design: no connection to customer databases, generated fake data in Playgrounds, no analytics.

    b) the SCCs, Module 3 (processor to processor), entered into between romulus and the Subprocessor, as incorporated in the Subprocessor's data processing agreement, together with any supplementary measures needed.

  2. Where the Customer is established in a country outside the EEA that does not benefit from an adequacy decision, and Data Protection Law applies to romulus's transfer of Customer Personal Data to the Customer, the SCCs, Module 4 (processor to controller), are incorporated by reference into this DPA, with the following options:

    • Clause 7 (docking clause): applies;
    • Clause 17 (governing law): the law of France;
    • Clause 18 (choice of forum and jurisdiction): the courts of France;
    • Annex I.A (list of parties): romulus as data exporter and the Customer as data importer, as identified at the top of this DPA;
    • Annex I.B (description of the transfer): Annex 1 of this DPA;
    • Annex II (technical and organisational measures): Annex 2 of this DPA.

  3. Switzerland. Where the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection applies to a transfer, the SCCs apply with the following adaptations: the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner is the competent supervisory authority for that transfer; references to the GDPR are to be understood as references to the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection; and the term "Member State" includes Switzerland, so that data subjects in Switzerland can enforce their rights there.

  4. United Kingdom. Where the UK GDPR applies to a transfer, the International Data Transfer Addendum to the SCCs issued by the UK Information Commissioner (version B1.0) is incorporated by reference. Its Tables 1 to 3 are completed with the information in this DPA and its Annexes, and either party may end it as provided in its Section 19.

  5. In case of conflict between this DPA and the SCCs, the SCCs prevail.
