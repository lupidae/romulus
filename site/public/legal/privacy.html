This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the hosted romulus service at https://romulus.lupidae.com (the "Service") and its website. It is provided under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the "GDPR") and French Law No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 (the "Loi Informatique et Libertés"). Capitalized terms have the meaning given in the Terms of Service at https://romulus.lupidae.com/legal/terms.

This policy is available in French at https://romulus.lupidae.com/fr/legal/confidentialite and in English at https://romulus.lupidae.com/legal/privacy. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails.

The Service is offered only to professionals (Section 3.5 of the Terms). The people whose data we process are therefore mostly people who use the Service for their work: freelancers and other professionals acting for themselves, and the staff and contractors of our business customers.

This policy does not cover installations of the open-source romulus software that others run themselves. Whoever runs such an installation is responsible for the data processed there.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Loi Informatique et Libertés, you have the right to:

  • access your data (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • rectify inaccurate data (Art. 16); you can also change your name in the sign-in service;
  • erase your data (Art. 17);
  • restrict processing (Art. 18);
  • data portability for the data you provided, processed on the basis of the contract (Art. 20). You can also export your content yourself from the Service at any time;
  • object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  • set instructions on what happens to your data after your death (Article 85 of the Loi Informatique et Libertés).

To exercise your rights, write to romulus@lupidae.com or to the postal address in Section 1. We may ask for information to confirm your identity if we have reasonable doubts. We answer within one month; this may be extended by two months for complex or numerous requests, in which case we tell you within the first month.

For personal data contained in an Organization's content or audit log, we forward your request to the customer concerned, which is the controller, and help it to answer.

Complaint. If you consider that your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, https://www.cnil.fr, or with the supervisory authority of the EU Member State where you live or work.

9. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • encryption in transit (TLS) between your browser or CI and Cloudflare, then through an encrypted tunnel between Cloudflare and our servers, which accept no incoming connection from the Internet;
  • isolation of each Playground in a gVisor sandbox without network access, with limits on processor, memory, disk and time;
  • session identifiers and API tokens stored only as hashes, and passwords stored only as hashes by the sign-in service;
  • role-based access control within Organizations, and administrative access to the infrastructure limited to a small number of named persons bound by confidentiality;
  • daily backups kept for 14 days, with restore tests;
  • logging of security events, and an audit log of sensitive actions in each Organization.

Details are in Annex 2 of the Data Processing Agreement. If a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we notify the CNIL within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and, where the risk is high, we inform you without undue delay (Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).
