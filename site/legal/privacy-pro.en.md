## 9. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

- encryption in transit (TLS) between your browser or CI and Cloudflare, then through an encrypted tunnel between Cloudflare and our servers, which accept no incoming connection from the Internet;
- isolation of each Playground in a gVisor sandbox without network access, with limits on processor, memory, disk and time;
- session identifiers and API tokens stored only as hashes, and passwords stored only as hashes by the sign-in service;
- role-based access control within Organizations, and administrative access to the infrastructure limited to a small number of named persons bound by confidentiality;
- daily backups kept for 14 days, with restore tests;
- logging of security events, and an audit log of sensitive actions in each Organization.

Details are in Annex 2 of the Data Processing Agreement. If a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we notify the CNIL within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and, where the risk is high, we inform you without undue delay (Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).
