- "**SCCs**": the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries adopted by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021.

## Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

**1. Encryption in transit and network exposure**

- All connections between users or CI systems and the Service use TLS, which ends at Cloudflare's edge. The website sends HTTP Strict Transport Security.
- Traffic between Cloudflare and the Service's servers goes through an encrypted Cloudflare tunnel opened from the servers. The servers expose no inbound port to the Internet.
- The API only answers requests that come through the website's edge, which adds a shared secret key.
- Emails are sent to the email provider, and data to Stripe, over TLS.
- Because TLS ends at Cloudflare, Cloudflare technically processes the traffic in clear at its edge. It is therefore listed as a Subprocessor.

**2. Playground isolation**

- Each Playground runs in its own container inside a **gVisor** sandbox, which intercepts system calls in a user-space kernel and separates the Playground from the host kernel.
- Playground containers have no network access, a read-only root file system, no Linux capabilities and no privilege escalation. Each is limited to one processor, 512 MiB of memory without swap, 128 processes, and a disk quota of 1 or 2 GiB depending on its size.
- Queries are limited to 30 seconds for reviewers and 120 seconds for developers; a migration run is limited to 10 minutes and 500 statements. The interactive console connects with a read-only role.
- Playgrounds are separate database instances, distinct from the Service's main database and from each other. They are filled with generated fake data, stop after 15 minutes without activity, are deleted 7 days after their last use, and are never backed up.
- The Playground database server logs no statements. Resource usage is monitored to detect abuse.

**3. Access control within the Service**

- Sign-in through a self-hosted identity service (Zitadel), using OpenID Connect with PKCE. Accounts are locked after 10 failed attempts.
- Sessions expire after 7 days without use and at most 30 days after sign-in. Session identifiers are stored only as hashes. Passwords are stored only as hashes by the identity service, whose keys are encrypted.
- Role-based access control within each Organization (administrator, developer, reviewer). Every request is checked against the caller's membership of the Organization and role.
- API tokens for CI are issued per database, with a scope, stored only as hashes, and can be revoked at any time.
- Rate limits protect sign-in and the main features against abuse.

**4. Administrative access**

- Administrative access to the infrastructure is limited to a small number of named persons, bound by confidentiality, and is used only to operate, secure and support the Service.
- Secrets are kept outside the source code, in files with restricted permissions on the servers.
- Physical access to the hardware is controlled by the hosting provider.

**5. Backups and resilience**

- The Service's databases are backed up every day. Each backup is checked after it is written and kept for 14 days. A backup is also taken before each deployment.
- Restore tests are carried out and their results recorded.
- Removed versions remain restorable for 30 days.

**6. Logging and monitoring**

- The application logs requests with their method, path (with tokens redacted) and identifier. It does not log the content of schemas, queries or comments.
- Each Organization has an audit log of sensitive actions, which cannot be modified or deleted through the application.
- Technical and security logs are kept for at most 6 months.

**7. Secure development and operations**

- The source code is public (AGPL-3.0), which allows external review. The Service runs container images built by continuous integration from identified commits, with pinned versions of its components.
- Vulnerabilities can be reported to romulus@lupidae.com under the security policy at https://github.com/lupidae/romulus/blob/main/SECURITY.md, which sets correction targets according to severity.

**8. Organisational measures**

- Confidentiality obligations for every person with access to Customer Personal Data.
- Written agreements under Article 28 GDPR with Subprocessors.
- A personal data breach procedure in line with Section 5.
- Data minimisation by design: no connection to customer databases, generated fake data in Playgrounds, no analytics.
